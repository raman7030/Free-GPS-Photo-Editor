🚀 Free Open-Source Field Visit App for Employees & Workers
I have developed a 100% free, fully open-source app designed specifically for field employees and remote workers.
Key Features:
• GPS Coordinate Editing: Modify location coordinates directly inside the app.
• Flexible Image Capture: Snap and tag both backdated and future-dated images.
• Custom Map Selection: Pick any location or choose directly from the interactive map.
• Multiple Templates: Choose from a variety of built-in templates to fit your workflow.
Note: This is a fully open project and free for everyone. It has been tested and works perfectly on my device, though it may still have minor bugs. If you need any support or want to contribute, I am always ready to help!
